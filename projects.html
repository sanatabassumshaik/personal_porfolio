02

MediAI

AI-powered Smart Healthcare and Hospital Management Platform.

Problem

Patients may struggle to determine how serious their symptoms are, which specialist they should consult and which hospital is most suitable. Appointment delays, waiting times and lack of personalized guidance can further complicate the healthcare experience.

Proposed Solution

MediAI is designed as an intelligent healthcare platform where users can create a health profile and enter symptoms using text or voice.

The platform can analyze symptoms, provide a probable disease assessment, categorize urgency and recommend an appropriate specialist.

It can also rank hospitals based on specialty, doctor availability, waiting time, ratings, distance and consultation cost.

Planned Features

  • AI-based symptom analysis
  • Emergency, urgent, normal and home-care categorization
  • Specialist recommendation
  • Hospital recommendation
  • Appointment booking
  • Queue waiting-time prediction
  • Medicine reminders
  • Digital health records
  • AI health assistant
  • Wearable device integration

Technologies

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript AI
